Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, tablet or mobile) when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to site owners.

What types of cookies do we use?

Necessary cookies

These are essential for the website to function. They allow navigation and use of different options or services that exist on it, such as controlling traffic and data communication.

Preference cookies

They allow you to remember information so that you can access the service with certain features that can differentiate your experience from other users, such as the language or region from which you access.

Analytical cookies

They allow us to quantify the number of users and perform statistical analysis of how you use our service. To do this, we analyze your navigation on our website in order to improve the offer of products or services we offer you.

Cookie management

You can allow, block or delete cookies installed on your device through the configuration of your browser options. Most web browsers offer the possibility to allow, block or delete cookies installed on your device.

Below we provide links on how to do it in the main browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note: If you block the use of cookies in your browser, some services or functionalities of the website may not be available.
